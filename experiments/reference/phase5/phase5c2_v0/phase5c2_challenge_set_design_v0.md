## 2. Split and access design

| Split | Purpose | Implementation access | Claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Challenge DEV | Debug, inspect traces/state transitions, identify obvious failures, validate behavior and tune proposed implementation within reviewed scope | May inspect DEV queries and DEV gold after construction; production process still receives query only | Development evidence only, never final confirmatory evidence |
| Challenge HOLDOUT TEST | Final common-input comparisons and predeclared attribution | Exact gold is evaluator-side and never accessible to the implementation agent. Exact queries preferably withheld from that agent until architecture/config are sealed; a separate runner receives query-only projections for execution | Independent confirmation within this constructed distribution, subject to uncertainty and audit validity |

A dataset custodian/legal annotation team may inspect TEST while building it, but must not also act as the implementation agent with retained TEST knowledge. Use separately permissioned storage, accounts/processes and agent contexts; hiding a filename in a shared readable workspace is insufficient. Runtime may receive a TEST query after implementation sealing; that permission never includes gold, difficulty, split dimensions or TEST scores. If exact TEST questions were exposed to implementers before sealing, record exposure and downgrade affected holdout claims or construct a fresh independent holdout before evaluation; do not call it untouched.

Scenario/template/proposition-bundle clusters, including paraphrases, stay entirely in one split. Broader legal-task families may appear in both splits, but exact scenarios and near-duplicate obligations must not bridge them. With the frozen corpus containing only three documents, this design is not document-held-out or external-law generalization. Article/source sharing across splits is permissible and reported; near-identical task/gold bundles are not. Whole-cluster allocation may force counts off target; solve feasibility before freeze rather than splitting clusters to hit counts.

## 3. Corpus-first construction procedure — future only

1. Lock corpus version and chunk/document/article identities. Custodian maps legal structure, actors, scope, conditions, evidence relations and known corpus limits without reading system answers or v2 outputs.
2. Build independent legal-task/scenario clusters from that map; bound user intent, relevance and expected response scope. The direction is **corpus/legal structure → legal task → query**. Known weaknesses may define dimensions, not hand-pick failures.
3. Draft candidates independently of v1/v2 answers; annotate and adjudicate gold from corpus before looking at any system retrieval/answer output. Maintain authorship and exposure declarations. No model-generated answer becomes gold.
4. Deduplicate against frozen questions, DEV/TEST clusters and the existing medical chatbot diagnostic. Exclude exact and trivial paraphrases of that diagnostic from HOLDOUT TEST; preferred policy excludes them from the entire new set to preserve fresh DEV confirmation too. The diagnostic remains **EXPLORATORY / NON-BENCHMARK / NON-CONFIRMATORY**. Custodian reviews exact and semantic near-duplicates locally in that later phase; no new query is written here.
5. Select using corpus dimensions, legal quality and predeclared cluster quotas; log every exclusion with a reason. Freeze selected text, gold and split assignment before empirical D6 characterization. No admission/exclusion based on v1 answer correctness, trigger frequency, v2 retrieval or evaluator outcomes.
6. A separate custodian may later execute the frozen initial retrieval base only, after construction lock, to characterize D6 against already sealed gold. Do not generate answers, tune queries, rewrite gold or replace items because Top-5 was unexpectedly sufficient. These future retrieval checks are not executed in 5C.2.
7. Audit schema, independent annotation agreement, applicability, contamination, cluster isolation and observed dimension coverage; seal dataset/version/hash/access manifests. Release DEV only to implementers. If a mandatory corpus-based dimension is infeasible, external review must amend the sampling design before freeze without reference to system quality.

## 4. Taxonomy and sampling matrix

Four broad sampling families provide an allocation frame: deployment/scope assessment; multi-duty procedural synthesis; actor/condition applicability; corpus-boundary/partial-answer handling. These are task categories, not instantiated queries or legal requirements. Each item has one primary family and any defensible dimension tags; each tag requires a corpus/annotation rationale. Broad wording alone is not proof of difficulty.

| Dimension | Operational definition before system outcomes | DEV minimum | TEST minimum | Tagging rule |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| D1 Broad / open-ended | Bounded deployment or legal-planning intent without an enumerated answer checklist | 4 | 8 | Annotate scope bounds; do not copy the diagnostic or invent unlimited requirements |
| D2 Multi-aspect | At least three independent substantive legal aspects under the scoped task | 4 | 8 | Independence reviewed; splitting one proposition into clauses does not inflate count |
| D3 Multi-article | Required supporting evidence spans at least two articles | 4 | 8 | Use adjudicated support alternatives, not retrieval ranks |
| D4 Cross-document | At least one valid full support bundle needs two or more corpus documents and no single-document alternative fully answers the scoped task | 4 | 8 | Must be verified corpus-first; coexistence of two retrieved documents is insufficient |
| D5 Dispersed evidence | No single chunk/article supplies all required support; support is separated across distinct locations | 4 | 8 | Report separation structure and alternative evidence bundles |
| D6 Initial Top-5 partial | Frozen initial Top-5 supports at least one but not every corpus-answerable required substantive aspect | 4 target* | 8 target* | Empirical characterization only after query/gold/split lock, using frozen initial base; zero coverage and full coverage are distinct |
| D7 Generation omission risk | Corpus contains sufficient support, but coordinated use of multiple independent propositions/exceptions makes omission structurally plausible | 4 | 8 | Annotate risk without observing generation; full evidence in actual final context is a separate post-seal mechanism-eligibility label |
| D8 Decomposition / reformulation opportunity | Task has at least two separable evidence needs or an ambiguity resolvable by targeted legal concepts | 4 | 8 | Record conceptual opportunity, never a v2-tested successful search string |
| D9 Applicability reasoning | Plausible keyword matches differ in scope, actor or a material condition | 4 | 8 | Include true-applicability and non-applicability/unknown-condition cases, not only traps |
| D10 Partial / insufficient corpus evidence | At least one material scoped ask lacks sufficient corpus support, including fully insufficient cases | 4 | 8 | Per-aspect sufficiency and query answerability adjudicated independently of Top-5 |

*D6's 4/8 is a desired exposure floor, not a guaranteed minimum that may be enforced by cherry-picking retrieval failures. All other minima are proposed corpus-based construction gates. D6 has `empirical_unverified` status until the later blind retrieval audit. If observed D6 falls below target, retain every selected item and report limited expansion exposure; no dimension-level confirmatory expansion claim. Any corpus-first enlargement requires external review and a separately versioned, independently constructed cohort, never output-guided substitutions or rewrites. No dimension count has been measured here.

Dimensions overlap; they are not mutually exclusive cells and minima must not be summed as independent sample sizes. The preferred 48-item design needs at least 120 dimension memberships at the proposed floors, about 2.5 tags per item, which is plausible but not proven feasible until corpus mapping. D4 often implies D3/D5; record the overlap rather than claim three independent tests. D6 and D7 can overlap when omissions are assessed after expansion; distinguish structural risk from observed final-evidence sufficiency. D10 can overlap D6 for a partially answerable query if its answerable subset is also only partly retrieved. Do not require D6 for a query with zero corpus-answerable aspects.

For D10, target at least 2 partially_answerable and 2 insufficient_evidence DEV items, and 4 of each TEST. For D9, target at least 2 DEV and 4 TEST instances each with a material unmet/unknown applicability condition and with conditions permitting supported application; these may be separate aspects in one item. Include within the main allocation at least 4 DEV and 8 TEST control cases where evidence search/repair is not inherently necessary, such as structurally adequate support or a clearly excluded candidate. Control opportunity is corpus-defined; actual no-trigger behavior is an outcome, not a selection rule.

## 5. Size and resource rationale

Preferred design: **60 candidates → 48 retained items: 16 DEV + 32 HOLDOUT TEST**, with **12 candidate reserves** used only for pre-output legal-quality/duplicate/cluster issues. Plan 15 candidates per primary family, yielding 4 DEV + 8 TEST + 3 reserves per family if feasible. Reserve membership inherits its cluster partition; exposed DEV reserves cannot later enter TEST. These are allocation targets, not real records. A candidate that passes may remain unused; do not run systems on reserves and pick the best benchmark.

The 48 retained count follows four task families with four DEV cases for inspectable positive/negative and applicability variants, and twice as many TEST cases to prioritize independent evaluation. Twelve reserves are a planning allowance for annotation attrition, not an observed 20% rejection rate. Range for review: 36 retained (12 DEV/24 TEST, lower subgroup resolution) to 60 retained (20 DEV/40 TEST, higher cost); candidate pool roughly 1.25 times retained, with legal-quality attrition uncertainty reported. The preferred size is a compromise, not a statistical power certification or a choice just because of round numbers.

At 32 independent TEST items, an illustrative worst-case binary-rate standard error is about 0.088 and a normal 95% half-width about 0.173. For an eight-item dimension, the analogous half-width is about 0.346. These are analytical planning illustrations under independence, not confidence intervals from observed data or estimates of power for paired continuous completeness. Cluster dependence reduces effective sample size. Large differences or repeated mechanism-localized patterns may be visible; small effects and rare strata can remain inconclusive. More production repetitions cannot replace more independent legal tasks.

Preferred main TEST workload: 32 items × 3 systems × 3 production runs = **288 trajectories**. Minimal two-run main workload = 192. Adding one attribution intermediate at the preferred three runs adds 96, giving **384 trajectories**. Using frozen-reference ceilings of one Standard LLM call and at most six per agentic trajectory, main logical LLM ceiling = 32×3×(1+6+6) = **1,248**; four-arm ceiling = 32×3×(1+6+6+6) = **1,824**. At most four retrieval calls per agentic trajectory gives **864** main / **1,248** four-arm retrieval calls, excluding separately logged merge-reranks. These are planning bounds conditional on unchanged budgets, not measured usage, HTTP attempts, token charges or quota availability. DEV runs, blind initial characterization, draft judging and transport recovery add cost and must be budgeted separately.

For annotation, two independent reviews of all 60 candidates imply **120 candidate-review units**, plus adjudication. Assuming 30–60 minutes per candidate per reviewer solely for planning gives 60–120 person-hours before adjudication, not measured legal annotation speed. Maintain distinct budgets for production calls, judge calls, CPU time and human review. If resources cannot fund the preregistered protocol, revise size/repetitions before TEST or report a non-confirmatory pilot; do not stop based on promising outcomes.

## 6. Gold schema design — fields only, no instances

Store gold in an evaluator-only dataset. A production projection is a separate strict object with only `query_id` (opaque) and `query` (exact original string). All other fields stay outside production. The following table describes types and semantics; it contains no records or required-point content.

| Object / field | Type / allowed values | Meaning and constraints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dataset identity | dataset_version, corpus_version, corpus_sha256, schema_version, annotation_protocol_hash, split_manifest_hash | Pin provenance and construction/annotation rules before system outputs |
| Item identity | opaque query_id; query string; query_hash; cluster_id; primary_family; split {DEV,HOLDOUT_TEST,RESERVE} | Query identity is stable; role and cluster metadata remain evaluator-side |
| Construction provenance | author/reviewer IDs, exposure declarations, timestamps, legal_structure_refs, exclusion/admission rationale | Demonstrate corpus-first construction and independence from system outputs |
| Scope | explicit_asks; bounded_interpretation; relevant_user_facts; unspecified_facts; excluded_scope with reasons | Prevent infinite gold for open-ended asks and unsupported factual assumptions |
| Dimensions | dimension IDs, rationale, annotation basis {corpus_structure,post_lock_initial_retrieval}, verification status | D6 remains empirical_unverified until future audit; labels not sent to runtime |
| required_legal_aspects | array of aspect definitions; aspect_id, proposition, origin {query_explicit,scope_necessary}, materiality, parent relationships | Finite legally reviewed answer obligations; scope_necessary requires direct intent and legal justification, not an LLM guess |
| Aspect answer role | substantive_supported; qualified_partial; unresolved_disclosure | Separate substantive points from response obligations to disclose a corpus limitation; do not award legal-point credit for merely saying unknown |
| Support bundles | array of alternative bundles; each contains all jointly necessary chunk/span links | OR across acceptable bundles, AND within a bundle; no need to cite every alternative. Store exact document/article/chunk IDs and text hashes |
| Evidence relationship | relevance {direct,contextual,irrelevant,uncertain}; role {normative_support,supporting_context,counterevidence} | Supporting context is an evidence role, not a legal applicability status |
| Applicability | {direct,conditional,not_applicable,uncertain}; scope/actor/condition predicates; {met,unmet,unknown,not_required}; uncertainty rationale | Direct means established predicates within the task; conditional must preserve assumptions; not_applicable cannot support a substantive duty |
| Evidence sufficiency | {sufficient_support,partial_support,no_support_in_corpus}; supported_scope; unsupported_scope; conflict flag and rationale | Sufficient means enough applicable evidence for the whole bounded aspect, including qualified conclusions when appropriate. Partial supports only a proper subset or leaves a necessary condition unresolved. No support is scoped to the locked corpus, never all law |
| Corpus audit trail | searched document/article coverage, alternative-bundle review, unresolved conflicts, annotation confidence | A no_support_in_corpus gold label requires documented corpus-wide scoped review, not failed Top-5 retrieval |
| Answerability | {fully_answerable,partially_answerable,insufficient_evidence}; derivation and rationale | Derived from material aspects and evidence as defined below |
| Response obligations | aspect_id, expected response mode, necessary qualification/disclosure type, acceptable equivalence rules | Schema only: future authoring is prohibited in this phase |
| Review | two independent assessments, disagreements, adjudicator rationale, final annotation status, locked_at | No system-output-derived gold. Reference answer is optional and, if later used, evaluator-only and not the sole scoring authority |

The suggested enum direct/conditional/supporting_context/not_applicable mixes applicability with explanatory function. Separate evidence role and applicability, and add uncertain for unresolved legal/factual application. Runtime additionally needs unassessed; finalized gold requires an adjudicated label, but uncertainty may itself be the correct annotation. Record contradictory evidence separately; do not silently label unresolved contradictions sufficient. Conditional gold may be sufficient for a conditional question, while a missing condition required for the user's concrete conclusion makes that concrete aspect partial.

**Answerability semantics:** fully_answerable means every material scoped legal aspect can be answered with sufficient applicable corpus support, including any correctly bounded conditional conclusion. partially_answerable means at least one material portion has support but at least one material requested conclusion remains unsupported/partial or blocked by missing applicability facts; answer the supported portion and disclose the rest. insufficient_evidence means no material legal portion can be substantively answered from the corpus for this scope; topical context alone does not make it partially answerable. Gold records required disclosure behavior without inventing external obligations. For each partial-support aspect, separately define a qualified-partial response obligation for its supported portion (Q in the evaluation protocol) and an unresolved disclosure obligation (U); neither gives full substantive-aspect credit in primary AC. Merely disclosing uncertainty cannot satisfy the supported-part obligation. Distinguish corpus insufficiency from the system failing to retrieve available evidence.

## 7. Annotation QA and freeze gates — future only

Two legal reviewers independently annotate scope, atomicity, support alternatives, applicability, sufficiency and answerability from the locked corpus. Reviewers are blind to system identities/outputs. Report raw agreement and confusion tables for applicability/sufficiency/answerability and matching agreement for aspect/bundle boundaries; adjudicate disagreements with documented sources. Serious unresolvable legal ambiguity leads to pre-output exclusion or an explicitly uncertainty-focused item with reviewed scoring, never outcome-based cleaning. Record both original annotations and adjudication.

Freeze gate: unique IDs; valid evidence links and source hashes; no orphan aspects; finite scope; gold response modes consistent with support/answerability; whole-cluster split separation; diagnostic/frozen-question contamination check; target coverage or approved documented shortfall; complete reviewer signoff; query-only projection schema and access plans; immutable dataset/query/gold hashes. D6 shortfall is recorded without substitutions and limits claims as above. No freezing or annotation occurs in 5C.2.

Post-freeze gold errors: preserve original version, blind-adjudicate suspected alternatives/errors without revealing system identity, publish a versioned amendment and sensitivity reporting applied to all arms. Never quietly edit gold to fit v2. If an error or disclosure compromises confirmation, report invalidity/limitations and use a fresh independent future test; do not tune and reuse TEST as untouched.
