# Research claim boundaries — resource-constrained challenge v0

Required label: **RESOURCE-CONSTRAINED MODEL-ADJUDICATED CHALLENGE BENCHMARK**.
Current status is an adopted protocol for a future completed benchmark. C
adjudication and final selection have not been performed in this amendment.
Independent human legal validation: **NOT COMPLETED**.

## Permitted, conditional claims

After the corresponding experiments and quality gates are actually completed,
results may describe comparative performance of frozen v1 and the authorized
final v2 on this independently constructed challenge cohort; challenge-specific
answer completeness/grounding differences; observed retrieval, generation and
controller failures; and observed mechanism behavior within the sealed design.
Do not assert any improvement, failure frequency or mechanism effect now.

“V2 performs better” must identify the benchmark, metric, denominator, uncertainty,
sealed comparison and applicable pre-TEST decision rule. It cannot be upgraded
to general legal superiority. Mechanism observations do not establish causal
effects beyond the experimental design; triggered subsets are descriptive and
post-treatment. A small cluster-dependent cohort supports limited subgroup
resolution. More repeated outputs do not create more independent legal tasks.

## Forbidden claims from this benchmark alone

- Expert-validated legal benchmark or independently human-validated legal gold.
- Definitive legal ground truth or definitive legal correctness.
- Population-representative coverage or generalization to all Vietnamese AI law.
- Expert-level legal reliability or two independent human legal reviewers agreeing.
- Causal mechanism claims beyond the experiment and its identification limits.
- An untouched confirmatory holdout when implementation exposure or access
  boundaries have not been controlled and validated.

Model-based A/B independence concerns separate annotation contexts, not legal
expertise or proof of uncorrelated errors. C is another model-based adjudication
pass; it can share model biases and misinterpret the same source. Evidence links
and structural validation establish traceability, not expert legal validation.
Corpus-v0.1 is a bounded evidence universe; missing support there is not proof
that no relevant law exists elsewhere. Current-law advice is outside this study.

## Required thesis/report disclosure

Only after all listed steps are completed, report:

> The challenge annotations were constructed through corpus-first candidate
> construction, two independent model-based annotation passes, A/B comparison
> and disagreement analysis, an independent model-based adjudication pass, and
> corpus-grounded evidence verification. They were not independently validated
> by a qualified legal expert. Results therefore describe comparative behavior
> on this resource-constrained challenge cohort, not definitive legal correctness
> or population-wide legal reliability.

Until then, mark C adjudication, evidence verification and final selection as
pending. Never copy this completion statement as a current accomplishment.
Report candidate attrition and reasons, disagreement normalization, unresolved
cases, family/dimension composition, cluster counts, denominator eligibility,
access-validation status and empirical D6 status without exposing HOLDOUT gold.
Disclose that annotation disagreement informed quality review, not selection
by expected system difficulty or by A/B agreement alone.

## Separate benchmark and decision contracts

The unchanged 31-query/102-point benchmark is the HISTORICAL COMPARABILITY
BENCHMARK. It is not a fresh v2 confirmatory holdout. Keep historical and challenge
metrics, denominators, uncertainty and conclusions separate; no pooled score.
Challenge query-level macro and pooled aspect metrics also remain distinct.

Acceptance margins are PROVISIONAL, with no final numeric value adopted here.
Finalize their decision and rationale at Phase 5C.5 before TEST, without access
to TEST quality outcomes for tuning. D6 remains EMPIRICAL_UNVERIFIED until after
final query/gold/split lock and its own later authorized blind characterization.
No initial Top-5, Standard/v1/v2 performance or evaluator outcome may influence
candidate selection, gold construction or post-hoc item replacement.

HOLDOUT_STORAGE_ISOLATION_NOT_YET_ENFORCED remains a blocker. Claim limitations
are not a substitute for the required access boundary. Final dataset freeze,
implementation and TEST execution are not authorized by this document.
